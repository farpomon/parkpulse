Delete your ParkPulse account

You can delete your account from inside the app under 👤 Account, or right here if you've already removed it from your phone.

How it works

Deleting sets a 7-day grace period. You're signed out immediately and we email you a cancellation link. Click that link — or just log back in — any time in those 7 days and your account carries on untouched. After that, everything below is erased for good.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and password
  • Saved trips, day plans and booking reminders
  • Your magical fairy conversations and everything she remembered about you
  • Wait-drop alerts and push subscriptions on this account
  • Every signed-in session, on every device

What we keep

Records of any purchase are kept for accounting and tax purposes, with your email address stripped out so they no longer identify you. Thumbs-up/down feedback you left on advisor replies is kept the same way — de-identified — so it still helps improve the product.

After 7 days, this cannot be undone.

If you have an active pass, deletion forfeits the remaining time once the grace period ends. There is no refund.
